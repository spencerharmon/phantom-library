Phantom Library

Surfaces the entire TMDB catalogue inside Jellyfin. Items materialise on demand via gostream's FUSE-backed virtual MKV files. Two layers of lazy loading: Phantom → Virtual → Materialised (this plugin) and gostream's pull-bytes-on-demand FUSE (gostream).
